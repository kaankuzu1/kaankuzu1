## Latest: [Dustling](https://dustling.app)

A free, open-source Mac cleaner. I built it after the usual cleanup apps failed to free enough space for a macOS update, so Dustling starts from one question: how much room does the update need, and how do I get there safely?

<a href="https://dustling.app"><img src="assets/dustling-home.png" alt="Dustling home screen: You need 20.2 GB more for the macOS update" width="850"></a>

- **Junk scan** for caches, logs, old installers and app leftovers, grouped by category with sizes.
- **App uninstaller** that removes apps together with their leftovers.
- **Startup items** you can switch on or off.
- **Nothing is deleted outright.** Files go to the Trash first, every run is recorded in History, and anything still in the Trash can be put back.

Swift 6 and SwiftUI, MIT licensed, macOS 14+, no telemetry or account. **[Download →](https://github.com/kaankuzu1/dustling/releases/latest/download/Dustling.dmg)** · **[Source](https://github.com/kaankuzu1/dustling)**

## Also building: [Audience Labs](https://audiencelabs.net)

Streamers sit on hours of VODs nobody rewatches. Audience Labs takes a Twitch or Kick VOD, transcribes it with Whisper, works out who is speaking and when the energy spikes, has an LLM pick the moments worth posting, scores them, and renders vertical clips with animated captions for TikTok, Shorts and Reels.

<a href="https://audiencelabs.net"><img src="assets/audiencelabs-landing.png" alt="Audience Labs landing page: Four hours live. Thirty seconds worth posting." width="850"></a>

Next.js, Python, Supabase and Modal. Open for beta requests: **[Request access →](https://audiencelabs.net/request-access)**

## Open source

**[clip-virality-scorer](https://github.com/kaankuzu1/clip-virality-scorer)** is the part of Audience Labs that decides which clips are worth posting, pulled out into a small Python package you can use on your own clips.

```bash
pip install "git+https://github.com/kaankuzu1/clip-virality-scorer"
clip-scorer rank hooks.json
```
